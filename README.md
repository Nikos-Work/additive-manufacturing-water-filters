# Additive Manufacturing of Mechanical Water Filters

## Overview
University project investigating the feasibility of using additive manufacturing to produce mechanical water filter structures. The study focused on CAD design, print quality, pore geometry, and alternative infill patterns for potential filtration applications.

## Objectives
- Design a multi-layer mechanical filter prototype based on the dimensions of a commercial activated carbon filter.
- Evaluate Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) and Vat Photopolymerization (VP) using an LCD resin 3D printer for producing fine-pore structures.
- Investigate different infill patterns as potential filtration structures.
- Assess print quality and identify manufacturing limitations.

## Design and Implementation
- Designed the filter geometry in SolidWorks.
- Explored a multi-layer structure with different pore diameters.
- Printed and evaluated prototypes using FDM and LCD resin 3D printers.
- Investigated gyroid, grid, and lines infill patterns at different densities.
- Adjusted FDM printing parameters to reduce stringing and improve print quality.

## Key Findings
- LCD resin printing provided better geometric detail for small openings, although some pores were obstructed by residual resin.
- FDM printing showed potential for producing mechanical filter structures, but print quality depended on the printing parameters and geometric details.
- Different infill patterns offered alternative internal structures, with trade-offs in geometry, flow paths, and manufacturing complexity.
- Material suitability remains an important limitation, as 3D-printing materials must meet the relevant requirements for contact with drinking water. In particular, the suitability of resin-based materials for this application requires further consideration.

## Tools and Technologies
- **CAD:** SolidWorks
- **Manufacturing:** FDM and LCD resin 3D printing
- **3D Printers:** Creality CR-10S Pro (FDM), Anycubic Photon (LCD resin printing)
- **Technical Areas:** Mechanical Design, Additive Manufacturing, Prototyping, Experimental Evaluation
